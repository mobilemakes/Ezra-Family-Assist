# Patient Rights Reference — Dialysis Patients (USA)

> This document summarizes key rights. Always consult a patient advocate or attorney for your specific situation.

---

## Federal ESRD Patient Rights (42 CFR § 494.70)

Dialysis patients receiving care at Medicare-certified End-Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) facilities have federally protected rights, including:

### Right to Informed Care
- Be fully informed about your health status, treatment plan, and any changes.
- Receive a complete explanation of procedures and their risks before they are performed.
- Know the name and credentials of everyone providing your care.

### Right to Participation in Care
- Participate in developing your care plan.
- Refuse treatment and be informed of the consequences.
- Request changes to your treatment schedule or dialysis prescription.

### Right to Privacy & Dignity
- Be treated with respect and dignity at all times.
- Have your personal health information kept private (HIPAA).
- Have visitors and family support present during treatment (subject to facility policy).

### Right to Grievance
- File a complaint or grievance with the facility without fear of retaliation.
- Receive a written response to your complaint.
- File a complaint with the state survey agency or CMS without the facility retaliating.

### Right to Access Records
- Request and receive copies of your medical records within 30 days (HIPAA).
- Review your dialysis treatment records, labs, and care plan.

### Right to Transfer / Discharge Protections
- Not be involuntarily discharged except in specific circumstances.
- Receive adequate notice and opportunity to find alternative care before discharge.

## HIPAA Rights (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)

- Access your medical records.
- Request corrections to inaccurate records.
- Know who has accessed your records.
- File a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS).

---

## Signs of Potentially Misappropriated or Substandard Care

Document and report if you observe:
- Sessions being cut short without medical justification
- Dialysis machine settings that differ from the prescribed plan without explanation
- Staff refusing to share treatment parameters or lab results
- Repeated missed medications or doses
- Failure to respond appropriately to complications (cramping, hypotension, access issues)
- Dismissal of patient or family concerns without documentation
- Pressure to not involve family members or advocates

## How to File a Complaint

### Facility Level
1. Request a meeting with the Facility Administrator or Medical Director.
2. Submit a written complaint and request a written response.
3. Keep copies of everything.

### State Survey Agency
Each state has an agency that oversees dialysis facilities. Search:
> "ESRD Network [your state]" or "[Your State] Health Department dialysis complaint"

### ESRD Network
There are 18 ESRD Networks across the US. They investigate patient grievances.
- Find your network: https://esrdnetworks.org

### CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services)
- File online: https://www.medicare.gov/care-compare
- Or call: 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227)

### Office for Civil Rights (HHS) — HIPAA Violations
- File online: https://www.hhs.gov/ocr/complaints

### State Medical Board
- For complaints against specific physicians.
